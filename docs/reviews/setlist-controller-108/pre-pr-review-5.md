## Approach decisions
- Pause by hand (#138): Nick's follow and link rule of 2026-10-24 stands. A fire event that answers no own command within `OWN_COMMAND_WINDOW_MS` is the lights' own only when it is the cue the live cue before it fires next by itself, from list reply arguments 19 to 21.
- Transport: OSC 1.0 packet-length framing on TCP 3032 (Nick, 2026-10-24 comment on #108), not SLIP.
- Liveness (#249, #257): an OSC ping, `/eos/ping` every 1000 ms, offline after 3 unanswered; connect timeout 3000 ms to Eos's first framed packet. The ping count resets on a complete packet only.
- Reconnect judgement (#254, review 3 finding 1, #268): the cue Eos is on at connect is judged once against the live cue kept from before the drop (`before`), the cue numbers of the lights' fires by number still unanswered at the close (`beforeOwn`), and the `firedAfter` chain of each. Both are cleared together by the lights' own command (`sendOwn`), a fire event, and the judgement itself; both survive a failed connect. Go and Back are not kept, by #268's scope (finding 1 asks for their own ticket rather than an in-place fix).
- Shadowed cues (#252, #258): the check never calls a cue that fires "never fires". The exemption is every cue that a follow or hang of any cue reaches, with the false negative #258 accepted.
- File check shape (#256 and the #84 merge): a name-matching target answers `FileCheck { unmatched, problems }`; problems render first with no heading, names under "Cannot match:". ProPresenter keeps every line under `unmatched`, as #84 shipped; moving its "no playlist picked" line to problems is the stage-2 proposal in `.epic-loop/stage2/proposals.md`, not this branch.
- New tests of the adapter drive the real server through `lightsRig` in `test/eos.test.ts` and the fake Eos, never reaching inside the adapter, as the epic's testing decisions require. Row behaviour that needs effects to run is tested in Playwright (`e2e/lights.spec.ts`).

## Confirmed Issues
### 1. A Go or Back whose fire event a drop eats pauses the lights by hand on reconnect — LOW
- **Location:** `src/server/eos.ts:423` (the close keeps only fires by number), judged at `src/server/eos.ts:292-294`.
- **Attribution:** introduced. The reconnect judgement came with #254 (`8c04f3a` "setlist-controller#254 Lights: a cue fired at the console during a drop pauses the lights on reconnect"); #268 (`68ad6ab`) kept the unanswered fires by number and, by its scope, not Go or Back (`git log -S'beforeOwn.add' f264357..HEAD` gives `68ad6ab`). The `origin/main` base has no lights at all. #268's own body says "The same path holds for the app's Go and Back", and its implementer filed it as a proposal (`.epic-loop/tickets/268/proposals.md`, "Lights: a reconnect after the producer's Go or Back, with its fire event lost, pauses them by hand"); #268's review kept it as F2.9, deferred.
- **Real-world trigger:** the producer presses Go (the timing is off, or the lights look stuck during a Wi-Fi blip at FOH), Eos receives it and fires the next cue, and the connection drops before Eos's fire event reaches the server: a silent drop that TCP delivers the Go through just before the lights give the connection up. On reconnect the row shows "Paused by hand" though nobody touched the console, and the lights fire nothing until the producer arms them. LOW, capped: it needs a Go or Back in the same fraction of a second as a drop, and a Go is a rare press where a section's fire is every 30 seconds (which is why #268's case was worth its ticket). Upgrade to MEDIUM if #140's acceptance run or a rehearsal shows the producer using Go or Back routinely, or drops at FOH are frequent.
**Evidence**
```ts
      for (const { command } of own) if (typeof command === 'object') beforeOwn.add(command.cue);
      own = [];
```
```ts
      const liveBefore = kept !== null && (number === kept || firedAfter(kept).has(number));
      const ownBefore = [...keptOwn].some((cue) => cue === number || firedAfter(cue).has(number));
      if ((kept !== null || keptOwn.size > 0) && !liveBefore && !ownBefore) reporter?.changedByHand();
```
**Failure mode** — armed on cue 3 of the recorded show; the fake is silenced (Eos acts, the lights hear nothing); the producer presses Go (Eos fires 3.5) or Back (Eos goes to 2); the connection goes silent and drops; on reconnect the connect burst names 3.5 or 2. `kept` is "3", `keptOwn` is empty because the Go or Back was thrown away at the close, and neither 3.5 nor 2 is in `firedAfter('3')`, so `changedByHand()` runs. Proved: `sh docs/reviews/setlist-controller-108/repro/review-5-repro.sh` runs "review 5 finding 1" for both buttons, red at HEAD with Received `"arm": "paused", "pause": "by-hand"`.
**Recommended fix** — no in-place fix on this branch: take #268's filed proposal as its own `ready-for-agent` ticket. Go and Back carry no cue number when sent, so the fix has to predict the cue Eos picks: for Go, the pending cue (the live cue's link, else the next cue in list order); for Back, the cue before the live cue in list order, or nothing when a fade is running (Back then only stops it, `test/fixtures/eos/back-during-fade.json`). Predict it in `runButton` at send time from the live cue and `cues`, carry it on the `Own` entry (for example `{ button: 'go', expects: '3.5' }`), and add it to `beforeOwn` at the close beside the fires by number. When no prediction is possible (no live cue, no list), keep nothing, which is today's behaviour.
**Remediation brief**
- **Design intent:** `beforeOwn` holds the cues the lights asked Eos to be on whose answer the drop may have eaten; on reconnect, the cue Eos is on is the lights' own if it is one of those or one that one of those runs on to. Go and Back are the lights' own commands too (they never pause, #137), but Eos picks their cue, so today nothing is kept for them. The judgement must still pause on a cue fired at the console during the drop.
- **Full surface:** `src/server/eos.ts:126` (the `Own` type), `:172-178` (`sendOwn`), `:183-191` (`answers`, whose matcher at `:273` treats any non-object command as accounting for any cue, and at `:279` matches `'back'` by identity), `:486-489` (`runButton`), `:423` (the close). - **Must preserve:** a cue fired at the console during a drop after an unanswered Go still pauses (finding 2's "review 5 g" test); Go and Back's own fire and stop events never pause and are used up through `answers` (the Go and Back tests near `test/eos.test.ts:955`); Back during a fade answered by a stop of cue 0 (`:279`); every test in `test/eos.test.ts`, `test/connections.test.ts` and `test/osc-tcp.test.ts`.
